# Games and experiments by Tech Reality

Free games and side projects, separate from the CoreCrawl apps.

- **Silent Summit** is a free 3D browser game: build, transfer and climb through 10,000 landscapes.
  - Play in your browser: https://tech-reality.github.io/games/silent-summit/
  - Download for offline play: https://tech-reality.github.io/games/silent-summit/silent-summit.zip (unzip it, then open `silent-summit.html` in Safari or Chrome)

- **Moon Descent** is a free lunar landing game with six sectors, daily routes and personal best scores.
  - Play in your browser: https://tech-reality.github.io/games/moon-descent/
  - Download for offline play: https://tech-reality.github.io/games/moon-descent/moon-descent-v1.10.zip (unzip it, then open `index.html`)
